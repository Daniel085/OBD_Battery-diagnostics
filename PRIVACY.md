# Privacy Policy — OBD Battery Diagnostics

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

OBD Battery Diagnostics reads battery and vehicle diagnostics from your car
through a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter and shows them on your phone.

## What the app collects

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

- Diagnostic readings from your vehicle, session captures, capacity-test
  results and state-of-health history are stored only on your device.
- They leave your phone only if you choose to share them yourself, for
  example by exporting a report or a capture through the share sheet.
- Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth** is used only to connect to your OBD-II adapter.

## Your vehicle

The app only sends diagnostic read requests. It never writes to, codes or
clears anything in your vehicle.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
